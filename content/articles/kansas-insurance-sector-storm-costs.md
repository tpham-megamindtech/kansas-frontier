A steady drumbeat of hail and windstorms is reshaping the property insurance market in Kansas, pushing up premiums and prompting both insurers and homeowners to rethink how they manage risk.

Kansas ranks among the states most exposed to severe convective storms, and years of costly hail claims have led some insurers to raise rates, tighten roof-coverage terms, or pull back from certain areas. Homeowners increasingly face higher deductibles specifically for wind and hail damage.

"The storms are not new, but the cost of what they hit keeps going up," an insurance agent in Wichita said. Contractors report growing demand for impact-resistant roofing, which can qualify for premium discounts.

Consumer advocates urge homeowners to review policies carefully, warning that coverage details have grown more complex.
