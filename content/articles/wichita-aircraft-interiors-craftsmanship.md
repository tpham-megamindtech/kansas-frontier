Behind the sleek exteriors of Wichita's business jets lies a world of painstaking craftsmanship, where skilled workers transform bare cabins into custom interiors of wood, leather, and precision-fitted components for buyers around the globe.

Aircraft interior work blends fine woodworking, upholstery, and advanced engineering, all subject to strict weight limits and safety standards. Every cabinet, seat, and panel must be both beautiful and certified to withstand the rigors of flight. The result is a niche that rewards exacting craft.

"It is furniture-making, but every ounce and every screw has to meet aviation standards," one interior specialist said. Wealthy clients often request bespoke designs down to the finest detail.

The specialized work supports skilled jobs and adds significant value to the aircraft built in the region, a hidden but important part of the local aviation economy.
