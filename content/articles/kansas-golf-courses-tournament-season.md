Kansas golf courses are welcoming a busy tournament season, hosting a full slate of amateur, junior, and charity events amid rising interest in the sport across the state.

From municipal courses to championship-caliber layouts, the state's courses have seen renewed participation, part of a broader golf resurgence. Junior programs are introducing more young players to the game, while charity scrambles bring communities together for a good cause.

"Golf came roaring back, and it has held on to a lot of those new players," a course professional said. The season runs from spring through fall across the state's varied terrain.

Course operators are investing in their facilities and youth programs, betting that the renewed enthusiasm for golf will endure and continue to draw new players to the game.
