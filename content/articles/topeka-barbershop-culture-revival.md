The classic barbershop is enjoying a revival in Topeka, where a new generation of shops is pairing old-school craft, hot-towel shaves and straight-razor lines, with contemporary style and a strong sense of community.

The shops have become gathering spots as much as grooming destinations, drawing regulars who come for conversation as much as a cut. Barbers say the trade offers a stable career and a creative outlet, and enrollment in barbering programs has climbed.

"A good barbershop is a neighborhood institution. People tell you things in that chair," one shop owner said. Many of the new shops emphasize skilled fades, beard work, and a welcoming atmosphere.

The resurgence has also created demand for licensed barbers, and local schools report strong interest from students drawn to the craft.
