A growing number of Wichita salons are investing in training so their stylists can properly care for curly and coily hair, filling a long-standing gap that has frustrated clients with textured hair.

For years, many clients said they struggled to find stylists trained in cutting and styling their hair type, sometimes traveling long distances or relying on word of mouth. Salons are now sending stylists to specialized education and stocking products formulated for textured hair.

"Every client deserves a stylist who understands their hair, period," one salon owner said. The shift reflects both consumer demand and a broader industry reckoning with inclusivity.

Stylists who build expertise in textured hair say they are rewarded with loyal clients and a steady stream of referrals in an underserved market.
