As advanced beauty and aesthetic treatments proliferate across Kansas, regulators and industry professionals are debating how to ensure client safety without stifling a fast-growing sector.

The rise of med spas, injectables, and laser treatments has blurred the line between cosmetology and medicine, raising questions about who is qualified to perform which procedures and under what supervision. Licensed professionals have called for clearer standards, while warning against overly burdensome rules.

"We all want the same thing, which is clients who are safe and results that are real," one licensed practitioner said. Some states have moved to clarify scope-of-practice rules for aesthetic procedures.

Consumer advocates urge clients to verify credentials and to be wary of deeply discounted treatments, which can signal cut corners on training or safety.
