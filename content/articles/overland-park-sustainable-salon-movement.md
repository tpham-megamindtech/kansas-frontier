Salons across Overland Park are going green, adopting practices that cut waste and reduce the environmental footprint of an industry known for its single-use foils, plastic bottles, and chemical runoff.

Participating salons are recycling foils and color tubes, composting hair clippings, installing water-saving equipment, and switching to product lines free of certain harsh chemicals. Owners say clients increasingly ask about sustainability, and that greener operations can also lower costs over time.

"Our clients care what goes on their hair and what goes down the drain," one salon owner said. Some salons have joined national certification programs that audit their environmental practices.

Skeptics note that "clean beauty" claims are loosely regulated, and stylists encourage clients to ask specific questions rather than relying on marketing labels.
