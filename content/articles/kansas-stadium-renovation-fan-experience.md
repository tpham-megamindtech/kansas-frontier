Kansas universities are pouring resources into stadium and arena renovations, aiming to modernize aging venues and enhance the experience for fans who increasingly expect comfort and amenities alongside the action.

The projects include upgraded seating, improved concourses, new premium areas, and better technology, funded through a mix of donations, revenue, and debt. Athletic departments say the improvements are necessary to remain competitive in recruiting and to keep fans coming through the gates.

"Fans have more entertainment options than ever, and the game-day experience has to keep up," an athletics official said. Preserving the character and history of beloved venues while modernizing them is a delicate balance.

Supporters welcome the upgrades, though some fans express nostalgia for the older facilities and concern about rising ticket prices to help fund the work.
