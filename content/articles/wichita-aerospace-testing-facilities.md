Long before a new aircraft carries its first passenger, its structures and systems are pushed to their limits inside the specialized testing facilities that form a quiet but critical part of Wichita's aviation ecosystem.

Engineers subject wings to loads far beyond anything they will encounter in flight, cycle components through simulated lifetimes of wear, and expose materials to extreme temperatures. The rigorous testing underpins the safety record that makes modern aviation possible.

"We break things on purpose so they never break in the air," a test engineer said. The work generates mountains of data that feed back into design.

The region's testing capabilities are a competitive advantage, allowing manufacturers to validate designs and earn certification without shipping components elsewhere, and supporting a corps of specialized engineers.
