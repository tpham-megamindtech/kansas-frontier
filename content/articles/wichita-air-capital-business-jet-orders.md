Strong demand for business jets is filling order books at Wichita's aircraft manufacturers, reinforcing the city's long-held claim as the Air Capital of the World and steadying an industry famous for its cyclical swings.

Wichita has built aircraft for more than a century, and a large share of the world's general aviation and business aircraft trace their roots to factories along the Kansas plains. Manufacturers report solid backlogs for light and midsize jets, driven by corporate buyers and fractional ownership programs.

"When the order book is full, you can feel it across the whole city," said an analyst who tracks the local aerospace sector. The strength has translated into hiring, overtime, and investment in production lines.

### Cyclical memories linger

Industry veterans remain cautious, mindful that aviation demand can turn quickly with the broader economy. Still, a diversified customer base and steady deliveries have given manufacturers reason for optimism. Suppliers across the region, from machine shops to interior specialists, are feeling the benefit of the sustained demand.
