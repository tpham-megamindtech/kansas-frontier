Women's college basketball is drawing bigger crowds and greater attention at Kansas programs, part of a national surge of interest that has transformed the profile of the sport.

Attendance has climbed, television audiences have grown, and young fans increasingly fill arenas to watch the state's college teams. Athletic departments have responded by investing in the programs and marketing games more aggressively.

"The energy in the building for women's games is completely different than it was a few years ago," an athletics official said. Players have become role models for a generation of girls in youth leagues across the state.

Advocates say the momentum reflects both the quality of play and a broader cultural shift, and they expect the growth in interest to continue building.
