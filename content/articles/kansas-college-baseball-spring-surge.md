College baseball in Kansas is swinging into a promising spring, with programs across the state drawing fans to the ballpark and developing talent under the warming prairie sun.

The sport offers a different rhythm than the frenzy of football and basketball, unfolding over long series across the season. Kansas programs compete in strong conferences and have sent players on to the professional ranks, giving local fans plenty to follow.

"Baseball season means spring is here, and there is nothing better than an afternoon at the ballpark," a fan said. Warm-weather road trips and doubleheaders are staples of the schedule.

Coaches emphasize player development and the grind of a long season, and they say strong local support helps sustain the programs through the ups and downs of spring baseball.
