Day spas in Wichita are broadening their offerings, blending traditional beauty treatments with whole-body wellness services as clients increasingly seek relaxation and self-care alongside skincare and massage.

Menus now feature infrared saunas, contrast therapy, guided breathing sessions, and lounges designed for unhurried downtime. Owners say the pandemic-era focus on wellness reshaped what clients expect from a visit, turning the spa into a retreat rather than a quick appointment.

"People are not just buying a facial. They are buying an hour to breathe," a spa director said. The expanded services also encourage clients to stay longer and return more often.

Practitioners stress that wellness offerings should complement, not replace, evidence-based skincare, and they encourage clients to keep realistic expectations.
