Hair and makeup artists in Topeka are booking months in advance as wedding season returns in force, one of the busiest and most lucrative stretches of the year for beauty professionals across northeast Kansas.

Bridal work is a specialty of its own, requiring artists to deliver long-lasting looks under pressure and often on location. Many professionals offer trial sessions, coordinate large wedding-party bookings, and travel to venues, from downtown ballrooms to rural barns.

"A wedding day is high stakes, and you have one chance to get it right," one bridal artist said. Demand is strong enough that top artists say they turn away work during peak weekends.

Professionals advise couples to book early and to schedule trials well ahead of the date, especially for popular late-spring and autumn weekends.
