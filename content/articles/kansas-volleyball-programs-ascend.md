Collegiate volleyball has become a growing point of pride in Kansas, as programs climb the national rankings and draw energetic, growing home crowds to their matches.

The sport has surged in popularity and competitiveness, and Kansas teams have earned national recognition and postseason berths. Strong recruiting, dedicated coaching, and passionate fan support have fueled the rise, filling arenas on match nights.

"The atmosphere at a big volleyball match now rivals anything on campus," a fan said. Young players in club and high school programs across the state look up to the college stars.

The growth mirrors a national volleyball boom, and Kansas programs have positioned themselves to compete with the best, giving the state another sport to rally behind.
