Track and field programs in Kansas are sprinting onto the national stage, with collegiate athletes competing for conference and national honors and extending a proud tradition in the sport.

The state's universities have long produced strong track and field teams, and the annual relays hosted in Kansas draw elite competitors from across the country. Coaches point to strong recruiting, modern facilities, and a culture that values the sport's blend of individual and team achievement.

"Track rewards discipline and consistency, and our athletes buy into that," a coach said. Success spans sprints, distance, jumps, and throws.

The sport's relatively low profile compared with football and basketball belies its depth in Kansas, where a rich history of standout performances continues to inspire the next generation of athletes.
