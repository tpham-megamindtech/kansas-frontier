Specialized studios devoted to lashes and brows are multiplying across Overland Park and the surrounding Johnson County suburbs, part of a national boom in services that promise low-maintenance enhancement of the eyes.

The studios offer lash extensions, lifts, brow lamination, and tinting, treatments that clients say save time on daily makeup routines. Technicians undergo specialized training and certification, and the niche has become a viable career path for many beauty professionals.

"Clients love waking up ready to go," said the owner of one studio. The precise, detailed work commands premium prices and encourages repeat visits for fills and touch-ups.

Professionals emphasize safety, noting that proper technique and patch testing are essential to avoid irritation around the sensitive eye area.
