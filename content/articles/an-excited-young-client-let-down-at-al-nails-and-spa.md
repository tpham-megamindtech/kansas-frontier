For a child, a nail appointment is a big deal, and the visit is measured as much by how she feels as by how the nails look. That's what makes this report on AL Nails & Spa hard to read: a little girl arrived excited, and by the end she was, in her parent's words, so sad. The problems were ordinary and fixable, but together they let a young client down on nearly every front.

## An Appointment That Didn't Come First

The family had made an appointment, yet the visit didn't reflect it. They waited about 20 minutes just to be seated, then the girl was left soaking for 45 minutes. Meanwhile, two people who arrived after them were taken care of first. As the parent fairly put it, being busy is understandable, but then what was the point of making an appointment? A booking is a promise of priority, and honoring it on time is the whole reason clients make one.

## A Young Client Treated Harshly, and a "No" Ignored

The service itself missed the mark and, more importantly, so did the way the child was treated. She didn't receive a full manicure, was told her nails were too little, and was shouted at not to move, which is no way to speak to a nervous young client. The salon also insisted on fixing a small spot on her toes even after the family repeatedly said no thank you, then buffed off the entire pedicure and redid it. The whole visit stretched to three hours, and the polish chipped and was gone within two days. For an excited first-timer, it added up to a confusing, disappointing experience rather than a fun one.

## Editorial Perspective

Every issue here is fixable, and getting them right is exactly what builds a salon families return to. First, honor appointments: seat and serve booked clients on schedule, ahead of walk-ins, because that priority is what the booking is for. Second, be especially gentle and patient with children. A young client should be met with warmth and encouragement, never a raised voice, and small hands call for extra care, not frustration. Third, respect a clear "no." If a family declines an extra step, that's the end of it, never a reason to redo work they didn't ask for. And finally, deliver the full service with lasting quality. A salon that makes a child's visit fun, gentle, and on time earns not just that client but her whole family for years. Handled with a little kindness and respect for the appointment, this excited little girl would have skipped out happy instead of sad.
