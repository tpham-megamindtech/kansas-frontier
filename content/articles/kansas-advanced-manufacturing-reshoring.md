A broad push to bring manufacturing back to the United States is benefiting Kansas, as companies seeking to shorten supply chains rediscover the advantages of the state's skilled workforce and central location.

The reshoring trend, accelerated by pandemic-era disruptions and geopolitical tensions, has prompted firms to reconsider overseas production. Kansas offers proximity to national markets, established industrial expertise, and, boosters argue, a reliable operating environment.

"Companies learned that resilience has value, and that value often points back to places like Kansas," an economic development official said. New and expanded facilities have followed in several sectors.

Analysts caution that reshoring is gradual and selective rather than a wholesale reversal, but say Kansas is well placed to capture a share of the production returning to American soil.
