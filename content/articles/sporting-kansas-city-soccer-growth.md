Professional soccer has built a devoted following in the Kansas City region, and its popularity is fueling a surge in youth participation that reaches well into Kansas.

The pro club's success and modern stadium have helped make soccer a mainstream part of the local sports landscape, drawing sellout crowds and inspiring young players. Youth clubs across Johnson and Wyandotte counties report growing enrollment, with children eager to emulate the professionals.

"Kids here are picking up a soccer ball the way earlier generations picked up a football or a basketball," a youth coach said. The growth has spurred investment in fields and academies.

Supporters say the region has become a genuine soccer hotbed, and the sport's rising profile is reshaping how many young Kansans spend their weekends.
