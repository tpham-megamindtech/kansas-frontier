Resort and hotel spas across Kansas are courting local guests with weekend packages, betting that residents are eager for short, restorative getaways close to home rather than distant vacations.

The packages typically bundle spa treatments with lodging, dining, and amenities, marketed as an accessible luxury for busy Kansans. Properties near lakes and in scenic areas have leaned into the trend, positioning themselves as retreats within an easy drive.

"You do not need to fly across the country to unplug for a weekend," a spa manager said. Local getaway demand has helped some properties fill rooms during slower stretches.

Hospitality analysts say the appeal of nearby, affordable escapes has grown, and spas have become a key draw for properties looking to differentiate themselves.
