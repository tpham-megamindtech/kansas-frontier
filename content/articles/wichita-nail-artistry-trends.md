Nail salons in Wichita are becoming showcases for artistry, as technicians push the boundaries of the craft with intricate hand-painted designs, structured manicures, and finishes that last for weeks.

The city's nail artists say clients increasingly treat their nails as a form of personal expression, requesting everything from minimalist looks to elaborate seasonal themes. Advances in gel and structured overlay techniques have made manicures more durable, a selling point for busy clients.

"It is wearable art, and every set is a little different," one nail technician said. Social media has raised expectations, with clients arriving with detailed inspiration.

Professionals stress the importance of proper sanitation and technique, and they encourage clients to choose salons that follow strict hygiene standards.
