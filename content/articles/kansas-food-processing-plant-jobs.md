Meat and grain processing plants remain among the largest employers in many rural Kansas towns, anchoring local economies and, in the process, reshaping the demographics of the communities around them.

In places like Garden City, Dodge City, and Liberal, sprawling packing plants employ thousands and draw workers from around the world, giving southwest Kansas some of the most diverse populations in the state. The plants provide steady, if demanding, jobs and support a web of local businesses.

"When the plant does well, the whole town does well," a community leader said. The facilities also present challenges, from housing shortages to strains on schools and services.

Industry officials say continued investment in automation and safety is reshaping the work, even as the plants remain fundamentally reliant on a large human workforce.
