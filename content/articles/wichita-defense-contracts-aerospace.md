Defense contracts for aircraft modification, sustainment, and support are adding ballast to Wichita's aviation economy, providing steady work that helps balance the notorious volatility of the commercial market.

Military programs often span years or decades, offering manufacturers and suppliers a predictable stream of work that can sustain employment through downturns in business jet demand. Wichita firms perform specialized modifications, refueling and support aircraft work, and ongoing maintenance for military fleets.

"Defense work does not swing the way the commercial market does, and that stability matters," an industry official said. The contracts also demand rigorous security and quality standards.

Analysts say a healthy mix of commercial and defense work makes the region's aviation sector more resilient, cushioning the blows when one side of the market softens.
