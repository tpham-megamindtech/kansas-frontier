A crop of Kansas entrepreneurs is building homegrown beauty brands, formulating skincare and cosmetics with an emphasis on simple ingredients, some sourced from the state's own farms and prairies.

These small businesses, often started in home kitchens before growing into commercial operations, market products made with ingredients like sunflower oil, beeswax, and botanical extracts. Founders say customers appreciate transparency about what goes into the products and where it comes from.

"We wanted to prove you can build a beauty brand from the middle of the country," one founder near Lawrence said. The brands sell online and through boutiques, farmers markets, and salons.

Building a beauty brand is fiercely competitive, industry watchers note, but a compelling local story and quality formulations can help small players carve out loyal followings.
